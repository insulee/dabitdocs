#### What is remote support?

With remote support, our staff see your screen through the RustDesk program. We diagnose and solve the problem in real time. You get fast technical support without a site visit.

#### Download and install RustDesk

1. **Download RustDesk**
   - Go to the [official RustDesk download page](https://rustdesk.com/)
   - Download the version for your operating system (Windows, macOS, Linux)
   - For Windows: click the Windows download button

2. **Install and run**
   - Run the installer that you downloaded
   - After installation, start RustDesk
   - You can use it right away. You do not need to sign up or log in

#### How to connect for remote support

When you start RustDesk, your **ID** and **One-time Password** appear on the left side of the screen. To get remote support, tell our staff the following information.

1. **ID** (9 digits)
   - Example: 123 456 789
2. **One-time Password** (6 characters)
   - Example: abc123
   - RustDesk makes a new password each time you start the program

#### How to request remote support

1. **Before you call**
   - Download and start RustDesk
   - Check your ID and One-time Password
   - Keep the problem in a state that we can reproduce

2. **Request support**
   - Ask the Dabit Solution technical team for remote support by phone or email
   - Tell our staff your ID and One-time Password
   - When the connection request comes, click **Accept**

3. **During remote support**
   - Our staff see your screen, then diagnose and solve the problem
   - If necessary, our staff control your mouse and keyboard (only with your permission)
   - You can watch the troubleshooting in real time

#### Security

To protect your information, please note the following.

- A connection never starts until you click **Accept**
- The connection ends automatically when the session ends
- The One-time Password becomes invalid when you close the program
- You can disconnect at any time during remote support
