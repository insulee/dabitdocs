---
title: 3.3. Making and Showing Background Images
---

> [!abstract] How to make background images and show them on the LED modules

#### How to make and send background images
1. 2.7.1. Making Images: make the image that you want with the Paint program, and save it as a .bmp/.jpg/.gif file
2. 2.7.2. Converting Images or Text to dat or ani Files: convert the image to a .dat or .ani file to load it in DabitOne
3. 2.7.3. Making a Playlist: save the .dat and .ani files as background images (.bgp), and upload them to the controller

#### How to show the background images uploaded to the controller
 - Show a background image together with real-time text or page text
	 - HEX protocol
		 - DabitOne: `전송` (Send) > `HEX 프로토콜` (HEX protocol) > `배경이미지` (Background image) > `이미지1` (Image 1)
		 - DBPS / DabitChe: select the background image > `1번 이미지` (Image 1)
	 - ASCII: `/B001`
- Show a background image by itself
	- DabitOne: `전송` (Send) > `배경이미지` (Background image) > `이미지 1` (Image 1) > `전송` (Send)
	- DBPS: `설정` (Settings) > `배경이미지` (Background image) > `1번 이미지` (Image 1) > `전송` (Send)
	- DabitChe: `고급설정` (Advanced settings) > `Protocol Simulator(V6)` > `특수기능` (Special functions) > select the background image > `1번` (No. 1) > `전송` (Send)

#### How to show 2 or more background images on a schedule
- Available in DabitOne (legacy: DabitChe v2.3.0 or later, or DBPS)
- The background images show one after another on the registered schedule
- You can select up to 10 of up to 64 image files
- **(Important)** `보드 기능 설정` (Board settings) > `BH1(10핀) 모드` (BH1 (10-pin) mode) > `8Pin Input(BIT BGSch)`
- DabitOne: `고급` (Advanced) > `배경 스케줄` (Background schedule) > select the items > `전송` (Send)
- DBPS: `설정` (Settings) > `추가 기능 설정` (Additional function settings) > `배경이미지 스케줄` (Background image schedule) > select the items > `전송` (Send)
- ASCII protocol packet: `![0020abc def ghi!]`
	- `abc def ghi`: the background image numbers to show on the LED display
	- Example: `![0020001 009 015 030!]`: registers numbers 1, 9, 15, and 30 of the background image list in the schedule and shows them one after another
