We make controllers that show data from industrial sites on LED displays.


### 1. Company overview

Dabit Solution has made its own industrial LED display controllers and communication protocols since 2006. Together with the controllers, we provide communication protocols, software, and partner training.

We develop and produce the main controllers and related modules for LED displays. We are a B2B company that supplies LED display makers, system integration (SI) companies, and engineering partners across Korea.

### 2. Vision and core values

For about 20 years, Dabit Solution has built skills in device control, network communication, and sensor integration. With these skills, we aim to become **a smart technology company**. We are taking our experience with industrial LED displays into the smart home and smart city fields. We supply control hubs and integrated displays to construction companies and system integration partners.

- **Technology**: We develop our own protocol and controllers.
- **Partner growth**: We give technical documents and training so that our partners can use the products well.
- **Trust**: We make products that keep running in the field.

### 3. Core strengths

### DABIT Protocol and DABIT controllers
At the center of Dabit Solution are the **DABIT Protocol**, which we developed, and the DABIT controller series that uses it. With this protocol, you can integrate PLCs, PCs, IoT devices, and many kinds of sensors. Many partners use it to develop their own systems.

### A partner-centered B2B business model
As a rule, we do not sell directly to end users or give them technical support directly. We do not compete with our partners. We focus on supplying technology to them. We now do business with about 100 partners in Korea and abroad.

### Training for partner developers
Dabit Solution gives technical training to our partners' developers **for free**. The training covers system structure, the protocol, and how to use the software. We also provide **DABIT DOCS**, which has detailed technical documents, and source code for program development.

### Naver Smart Store
Together with B2B sales, Dabit Solution runs a **Naver Smart Store** (an online store in Korea). Small companies and the R&D departments of large companies can quickly buy small quantities of parts for prototypes there.

### Track record
In 2007, we supplied controllers to major national facilities for their parking systems. Since then, our controllers have been used for more than 15 years at many industrial sites, such as factory automation (FA) lines including semiconductor plants, intelligent transportation systems (ITS), and solar power plants.

### 4. Main business areas

- **Parking control systems**: We supply controllers for parking system LED displays in apartments, buildings, supermarkets, and department stores in Korea.
- **Intelligent transportation systems (ITS)**: We supply controllers for traffic facilities that need real-time data integration, such as illegal parking enforcement, road information, vehicle speed detection, and bus information systems (BIS).
- **Factory automation (FA)**: Our controllers are used at industrial sites, including semiconductor plants, for production status monitoring, forklift status displays, and more.
- **Environment and energy monitoring**: We build systems that show sensor data in real time, such as solar power generation, environmental data (fine dust, noise, water quality), and power supply and demand.
- **Custom solutions**: We develop products for the needs of new fields, such as serving robots, smart bus stops, and LED displays for special vehicles.

### 5. Technology and products

#### DABIT Protocol
Our own communication protocol for real-time message transfer at industrial sites. It supports both ASCII and HEX code commands. It can show up to 255 background images (text, graphics) by number.

#### Connectivity and expansion
- **LAN-to-serial converter**: The DB300E (wired) converter connects existing serial devices to a network.
- **Wireless option board**: When you attach the DBW320 to the controller, you can change the LED display text from a phone over Wi-Fi.
- **Option modules**: You can connect many external devices, such as temperature/humidity and brightness sensors, GPS receivers, Discrete Signal Input Boards for PLC integration, and RTC modules.

#### Integrated operation software: DabitOne
- The current PC operation software. It does everything from content creation (text and images) to schedule editing and sending to the controller
- Built-in commands and file transfer based on the DABIT Protocol (ASCII and HEX) — you can install and run a site without knowing the protocol
- Supports Serial, TCP, UDP, and MQTT communication and the full lineup of DABIT/DIBD controllers
- The new software that follows DabitChe, our previous operation program. **It is a separate program, not a new name for DabitChe.** Customers who use DabitChe can keep using it

### 6. Company history and future vision

### Founding and first supplies (2006–2009)
In 2006, when the company was founded, we started technology development for parking control and illegal parking enforcement. In 2007, we supplied the parking systems of major national facilities. We then expanded into other public facilities.

### Focus on controllers (2010–2024)
In 2010, we stopped making complete LED displays and decided to focus on **developing controllers and core parts**. In this period, we developed solar inverter integration technology and released new control systems such as the DIBD600 series.

### IoT controllers and new fields (2024–)
We widened our business by developing systems that integrate environmental sensors, such as fine dust, radiation, and noise sensors.
We released DIBD Protocol V7. We also developed **ESP32 controllers (DABIT300/502 series)** with Wi-Fi and OTA (over-the-air firmware update). Recently, we have been developing MQTT protocol integration and systems for serving robots and special vehicles.
