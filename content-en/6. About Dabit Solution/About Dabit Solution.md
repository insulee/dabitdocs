### 1. Company overview

Dabit Solution is a technology-driven small company. Its own protocol technology set the standard for the industrial LED control system market in Korea. Since the business started in 2006, Dabit Solution has grown beyond a simple hardware maker. It has built a unique technology ecosystem that covers controllers, communication protocols, software, and partner training.

We are a B2B company. We develop and produce the main controllers and related modules, which are the core parts of industrial LED displays. We supply them to LED display makers, system integrators (SI), and engineering partners across Korea. With more than 15 years of research and development and stability proven in industrial sites, we are now the clear market leader with **over 80%** of the protocol-based LED display controller market in Korea.

### 2. Vision and core values

For about 20 years, Dabit Solution has built skills in device control, network communication, and sensor integration. Based on these skills, we are moving toward a new vision: **a smart technology company**.
We use our leadership in the industrial control market to expand our B2B capabilities into the smart home and smart city infrastructure markets. This does not mean selling directly to consumers. It means that our B2B model grows: we supply core solutions, such as custom control hubs and integrated displays, to construction companies and system integration partners.

- **Technology leadership**: We develop and lead the technology that sets the market standard.
- **Partner growth**: We believe that our partners' success is our success. We fully support our partners with technology and training.
- **Absolute trust**: We earn the absolute trust of our customers with products that work perfectly in any industrial environment.

### 3. Core strengths

### Our own technology ecosystem and market standard
Our strongest advantage is the **DABIT Protocol**, which we developed, and the DABIT controller series that uses it.
The protocol works fully with PLCs, PCs, IoT devices, and many kinds of sensors. Many partners use it as the standard for their own system development.
This creates a strong technical lock-in that makes it hard to change to a competitor's product. It is the basis of our market share of over 80%.

### A partner-centered B2B business model
As a rule, we focus on B2B. We do not sell directly to end users or give them technical support directly.
This strategy prevents conflicts with our partners from the start. Our partners see Dabit Solution as a trusted technology supplier, not as a competitor. Our strong relationships with more than 100 partners in Korea and abroad are the core asset of our business.

### Strong partnerships through developer training
Dabit Solution gives in-depth technical training to our partners' developers **for free**. The training covers system structure, the protocol, and how to use the software.
This strategy goes beyond simple customer support. It helps our partners build the technology into their own skills. **DABIT DOCS**, which gives detailed technical documents, and the program source code that we provide make this strategy stronger.
This keeps our partners deeply integrated in the Dabit Solution technology ecosystem. It is a strong customer retention strategy and a barrier to entry for competitors.

### Smart sales channels for future customers
In addition to traditional B2B sales, Dabit Solution runs a **Naver Smart Store** (an online store in Korea) to reach more customers efficiently.
Through it, small companies and the R&D departments of large companies can quickly buy small quantities of parts for tests and prototypes.
The Smart Store is the top of an effective **sales funnel**. At low cost, it finds future large B2B customers and spreads our technology.

### Reliability and technology proven for more than 15 years
In 2007, although we were a new company, we supplied controllers for the parking systems of major national facilities that require the highest reliability.
Since then, our technology has been proven for more than 15 years in many industrial sites that require extreme reliability, such as factory automation (FA) lines including semiconductor plants, intelligent transportation systems (ITS), and solar power plants.

### 4. Main business areas

- **Parking control systems**: Our controllers are the de facto standard for LED displays in parking systems in apartments, buildings, supermarkets, and department stores in Korea.
- **Intelligent transportation systems (ITS)**: We provide solutions for traffic infrastructure that needs real-time data, such as illegal parking enforcement, road information, vehicle speed detection, and bus information systems (BIS).
- **Factory automation (FA)**: We are a key partner for industrial control technology in major industrial sites, including semiconductor plants. Examples are production status monitoring and forklift status displays.
- **Environment and energy monitoring**: We build systems that show sensor data in real time, such as solar power generation, environmental data (fine dust, noise, water quality), and power supply and demand.
- **Custom solutions**: We develop flexible and innovative solutions for new market needs, such as serving robots, smart bus stops, and LED displays for special vehicles.

### 5. Technology and products

#### DABIT Protocol
Our own communication protocol, optimized for fast, real-time message transfer in industrial environments.
It supports simple ASCII and HEX code commands at the same time, which makes development easy. With a simple number call, it can show up to 255 background images (text, graphics).

#### Connectivity and expansion
- **LAN-to-serial converter**: The DB300E (wired) converter connects existing serial devices to modern networks.
- **Wireless option board**: When you attach the DBW320 to the controller, you can change the LED display text from a phone over Wi-Fi.
- **Option modules**: Temperature/humidity and brightness sensors, GPS receivers, Discrete Signal Input Boards for PLC integration, RTC modules, and other external devices meet the needs of specific industries.

#### Integrated operation software: DabitOne
- The current PC operation software. It does everything from content creation (text and images) to schedule editing and sending to the controller
- Built-in commands and file transfer based on the DABIT Protocol (ASCII and HEX) — you can install and run a site without knowing the protocol
- Supports many communication methods, such as Serial, TCP, UDP, and MQTT, and the full lineup of DABIT/DIBD controllers
- The new software that follows DabitChe, our previous operation program. **It is a separate program, not a new name for DabitChe.** Customers who use DabitChe can keep using it

### 6. Company history and future vision

### Early growth and market trust (2006–2009)
In 2006, when the company was founded, we started technology development for the parking control and illegal parking enforcement markets.
In 2007, we completed parking system projects for major national facilities and earned strong trust in the market. We then quickly expanded into major public infrastructure.

### Strategic change and market leadership (2010–2024)
In 2010, we made a major strategic change. We stopped making complete LED displays and put all our capability into **developing controllers and core parts**.
This decision made Dabit Solution the market leader that sets the technology standard.
In this period, we led the energy market by advancing solar inverter integration technology. We also widened the technology gap with next-generation control systems such as the DIBD600 series.

### Advanced technology and new business (2024–)
We have diversified our business by developing systems that integrate environmental sensors, such as fine dust, radiation, and noise sensors.
We released DIBD Protocol V7. We also successfully **developed next-generation ESP32 controllers (DABIT300/502 series)** with Wi-Fi and OTA (over-the-air firmware update). This built the technology base for the IoT era.
Recently, we have secured future growth with MQTT protocol integration and with systems for serving robots and special vehicles.
