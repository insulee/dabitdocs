title: 2.7.4. Checking Playlists and Images Uploaded to the Controller
---

> [!abstract] How to check that the playlist (.pla) and background images (.bgp) that you sent to the controller went in correctly

> [!note] If you use the old DabitChe, see [[2.7.6. Sending Images from DabitChe]].

#### There is no download (read) function

- There is no function to bring the playlist (.pla) or background image (.bgp) files saved in the controller back to the PC (download or read).
- So check the uploaded content with the display tests below.

#### Check the playlist (.pla) — check the display

- When the transfer ends, the controller shows the playlist in the registered order.
- Even after you turn the controller power off and on again, the saved playlist shows in order.
- You can see which playlist is uploaded by looking at the screen.

#### Check the background images (.bgp) — call them by number

- `전송` (Send) tab > `배경이미지` (Background image) > select a number > `전송` (Send)
- If you send the numbers in order from 1, you can check which image is saved at which number.
