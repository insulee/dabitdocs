> [!abstract] How to arrange modules in ways other than the basic horizontal layout
> For the maximum size of modules for each controller, see [[3.2. Maximum Modules per Controller]]

### Where to set it
#### DabitOne
- `설정` (Settings) > `전광판 화면 구성` (LED display screen configuration) > `배열` (Layout)

#### DBPS
- `Setting` > `Display Size` > `Module layout`

#### DabitChe
- `환경설정` (Preferences) > `화면설정` (Screen settings) > `전광판 화면 설정 창` (LED display screen settings window) > `모듈배열방법` (Module layout)

### LED module layouts
#### 1. Horizontal (default)
- **Example 1: 6 modules of 1 row × 2 columns → 1 row × 12 columns**
	- Controller: DB500 series or DB502S
	- Settings
		- 세로 (Height): `1`
		- 가로 (Width): `12`
		- 배열 (Layout): `가로형(default)` (Horizontal)
	- Layout and display
		- ![[모듈배열방법1.png]]
- **Example 2: 2 modules of 1 row × 4 columns → 2 rows × 4 columns**
	- Controller: DB500 series or DB502S
	- Settings
		- 세로 (Height): `2`
		- 가로 (Width): `4`
		- 배열 (Layout): `가로형(default)` (Horizontal)
	- Layout and display
		- ![[모듈배열방법15.png|300]]
- **Example 3: 3 modules of 4 rows × 4 columns → 4 rows × 12 columns**
	- Controller: DB600T
	- Settings
		- 세로 (Height): `4`

		- 배열 (Layout): `가로형(default)` (Horizontal)
	- Layout and display

- **Example 4: 3 modules of 1 row × 4 columns → 3 rows × 4 columns**

#### 2. Vertical (1 row)

#### 3. Vertical (2 rows)
- Place 2-row modules one above the other to make 4 or more rows
- **Example 1: 3 modules of 2 rows × 2 columns → 6 rows × 2 columns**
	- Controller: DB600T
	- Settings
		- 세로 (Height): `6`
		- 가로 (Width): `2`
		- 배열 (Layout): `2줄 세로형` (Vertical, two rows)
	- Layout and display
		![[모듈배열방법6.png|230]]
- **Example 2: 6 modules of 1 row × 4 columns → 6 rows × 4 columns**
	- If the modules are large and many, use the vertical (2 rows) layout to keep the flat cables as short as possible and prevent data loss
	- Settings

		- 배열 (Layout): `2줄 세로형` (Vertical, two rows)

#### 4. Horizontal, double-sided
- Modules of the same size on both sides
- You can set up to half of the maximum modules, counted on one side
	- **V4.5.4 or later**: the column limit is fixed, so one side can have up to **half the column limit**, and rows up to the row limit — DB500S · DB600S 2 rows × 8 columns, DB600T 4 rows × 8 columns, DB600U 8 rows × 4 columns, DB520S 2 rows × 16 columns, DB720T 4 rows × 16 columns, DB720U 8 rows × 7 columns (column limits: [[3.2. Maximum Modules per Controller]])
	- **Before the V4.5.4 upgrade** (V4.0.3 or earlier · by total number of modules): the DB500 series up to 1 row × 20 columns or 2 rows × 10 columns, the DB600 series up to 2 rows × 20 columns or 4 rows × 10 columns
	- For the maximum layouts of the new DB520 · DB720, see [[3.2. Maximum Modules per Controller]]
- **Example 1: 4 modules of 1 row × 4 columns → 2 rows × 4 columns, double-sided**
	- Settings
		- 세로 (Height): `2`
		- 가로 (Width): `4`
		- 배열 (Layout): `가로 양면형` (Horizontal, double-sided)
	- Layout and display

	- Settings

	- Layout and display

#### 6. Horizontal (2 rows)
- Use it when you place 2-row modules side by side horizontally
- The sixth option in the DabitOne `배열` (Layout) combo (layout code `5` of the ASCII protocol screen size setting (40))

#### Note 1 - Horizontal vs. vertical (2 rows)
##### Settings for 4 rows × 6 columns made of 2 modules of 2 rows × 6 columns

- To make 4 rows × 6 columns with 2 modules of 2 rows × 6 columns, there are two ways:

| Controller         | Height  | Width  | Layout           |
| ------------ | --- | --- | ------------ |
| DB500S/DB502 | 2   | 12  | `가로형(default)` (Horizontal) |
| DB600T       | 4   | 6   | `2줄 세로형` (Vertical, two rows)       |

- The difference between the two is the display order. When you enter 24 characters in total, the characters show in this order:
	- 2 rows × 12 columns (horizontal)
		- ![[모듈배열방법10.png|350]]
	- 4 rows × 6 columns (vertical, 2 rows)
		- ![[모듈배열방법11.png|350]]
- Use this difference to choose the right controller and module layout.
