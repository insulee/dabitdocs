#### What is a quote request?

A quote request is a service to ask for the prices of Dabit Solution LED display products (controllers, LED modules, peripherals, and more). Enter the product information you need in the online form. Our staff review it and send you an accurate quote.

Fill in the online quote request form at the link below.

 → [Request a quote](https://forms.gle/SLbBPm4rAcWLYFg29) (form in Korean)

#### Products we quote

We provide quotes mainly for these products.

1. **LED display controllers**
   - LED display controllers: DB520, DB720 (new products in 2026), DB500, DB502, DB600, and more
   - LAN-to-serial converters: DB300E and more
   - Wireless option board: DBW320

2. **LED modules**
   - [[3.1. LED Module Information and Display Signals|See Dabit Solution LED modules]]

3. **Other products**
   - LED display kits
   - Distribution boards, Discrete Signal Input Boards
   - Sensors (temperature/humidity, fine dust, brightness, and more)

#### Information needed for a quote

To get an accurate quote quickly, please prepare the following information.

**Basic information**
- Company name and contact person
- Phone number where we can reach you
- Email address to receive the quote

**Information for each product**

**LED display controllers**
- Module rows and columns of the LED display (for example: 4 rows × 10 columns, 2 rows × 20 columns), or the total number of pixels
- Communication method you need (TTL, RS232, Ethernet, RS485, Wi-Fi, and more)
- Quantity

**LED modules**
- Pitch (pixel spacing): P10, P8, P6, P4, P3, and more
- Total size or total number of pixels
- Environment: indoor / outdoor
- Quantity

**Other products**
- Product names and quantities
- Functions or specifications you need

#### Quote process

1. **Fill in the form**
   - Open the quote request form at the link above
   - Agree to the collection and use of personal information
   - Enter the product information you need

2. **Quote review**
   - Dabit Solution staff review your request
   - We may contact you if we need more information

3. **Quote delivery**
   - We email the quote within 1–2 business days
   - The quote gives exact prices and product specifications
