title: 3.6. Updating the Firmware of the WifiBLE to Serial Converter
---

> [!Abstract] The setting changes and the procedure to update the firmware of DB300WB and DB320WB, with a specific example

> [!warning] DB300WB · DB320WB are discontinued
> - Use this document to maintain DB300WB · DB320WB units that are already installed
> - For wireless connection at new sites, use the **DBW320 wireless option board** — see [[2.1.6. Using the DBW320 Wireless Option Board]]

#### Example of setting changes

|               | DB300/320WB default setting            | Changed setting                       |
| ------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Wi-Fi SSID    | (default SSID — see the Korean document)                  | `dabit`                     |
| Wi-Fi Password | (default password — see the Korean document)                  | `dabit`                     |
| IP Address    | `192.168.0.201`             | `192.168.1.201`             |
| Firmware file        | `DB300_WIFI_BLE_V2.0.4.bin` | `DB300_WIFI_BLE_V2.0.5.bin` |

> [!warning] Use the default password only for the first setup
> All products have the same default Wi-Fi password. After the installation, **change the password**, as in the "Changed setting" column of the table.

#### How to update
1. Check the Wi-Fi settings
	- Command Prompt (cmd) > `ipconfig /all`
		- IPv4 Address. . . . . . . . . . . : 192.168.1.19(Preferred)
		- Subnet Mask . . . . . . . . . . . : 255.255.255.0
		- Default Gateway . . . . . . . . . : 192.168.1.1
2. Update the firmware
	- The PC steps below use the dbNet screen in the `통신` (Communication) tab of DabitOne (the legacy DabitChe/DBPS have the same flow)
	- Connect DB300WB/DB320WB
		- For AP mode or STA mode, see [[2.1.2. LAN-to-Serial Converter Settings]]
		- TTL (COM) connection: set the communication method on the left to a value other than `Serial`. In the `연결:` (Connection:) combo box at the top of dbNet, select the COM port > `Search` at the bottom
		- **Caution**: if the communication method on the left is `Serial`, the whole dbNet panel is disabled
	- `DB300` > select the `STA` radio button (choose `STA` or `AP`)
		- WiFi SSID: `dabit`
		- WiFi PW: `dabit`
		- `Set`
	- `Network` > 
		- IP Address: `192.168.1.201` (only the last part is different from the IPv4 address that you found with ipconfig /all)
		- Subnet: `255.255.255.0` (the same as the subnet mask that you found with ipconfig /all)
		- Gateway: `192.168.1.1` (the same as the default gateway that you found with ipconfig /all)
		- `Set`
	- Update the firmware with ElegantOTA
		- http://192.168.1.201/update
		- `Firmware` > `Choose File` > `DB300_WIFI_BLE_V2.0.5.bin` > `Open`
