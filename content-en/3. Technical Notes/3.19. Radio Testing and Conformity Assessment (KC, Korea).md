> [!abstract] Dabit Solution products that passed radio testing and conformity assessment, and the related documents

- We understand that radio testing and conformity assessment of the Dabit Solution controllers and converters are necessary when you certify a finished LED display
- Dabit Solution DB502S and DB300E (V06) passed radio testing and conformity assessment. However, this does not guarantee that the finished LED display passes conformity assessment
- So the finished LED display as a whole must also be assessed

#### Controllers and converters

 To meet the certification requirements for a finished LED display, buy the products below, which completed radio testing and conformity assessment

1. **DB502S+DB300E(V06)**
2. **DB600S**

#### Radio testing and EMC conformity assessment by product

##### DB502S+DB300E(V6)
- Certification status:  radio testing and EMC conformity assessment completed
- Description: DB502S and DB300E (V06) are products that received radio testing and EMC conformity assessment
##### DB600S
- Certification status: passed EMC conformity assessment
- Description: DB600S passed EMC conformity assessment and gives stable performance in many LED display environments

#### Product choice and price

- DB502+DB300E(V06) and DB600S have the same price
- Choose one of the two products by your requirements and environment
- Both products meet the radio testing and EMC conformity requirements
