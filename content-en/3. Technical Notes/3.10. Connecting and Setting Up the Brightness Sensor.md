---
title: 3.10. Connecting and Setting Up the Brightness Sensor
---

> [!abstract] How to connect and set up the brightness sensor for automatic brightness control of the LED display


#### Connecting the brightness sensor
- Connect the 2-pin cable of the brightness sensor to GND and D1 of the J4 RELAY port on the controller

| Controller J4 Relay | Brightness sensor  |
| ------------- | ----- |
| GND           | Black wire |
| D1            | Red wire |

#### DabitOne
- `고급` (Advanced) > `보드기능설정` (Board settings) > `J4(4핀) 모드: CDS` (pin mode) > `전송` (Send)
- In the `전송` (Send) tab, `밝기` (Brightness) group > `고급설정` (Advanced settings)
	1. Select the CDS연동 (CDS link) radio button
	2. Click `CDS 읽기` (Read CDS)
	3. Turn the controller power off and on
	4. Enter the minimum and maximum of 조도범위 (light level range)
	5. Enter the lowest and highest of 밝기범위 (brightness range)
	6. `적용` (Apply)

#### DabitChe
- `고급설정` (Advanced settings) > `보드기능설정` (Board settings) > `J4(4pin) Function: CDS` > `저장` (Save)
- `환경설정` (Preferences) > `휘도설정` (Luminance settings)
	1. Select the 자동 설정(CDS센서연동) (auto setting, CDS sensor link) check box
	2. Click `CDSRead`
	3. Turn the controller power off and on
	4. Enter the minimum and maximum of the CDS (brightness sensor) reading
	5. Enter the lowest and highest LED module brightness (%)
	6. `전송` (Send)

> [!tip] New controllers (firmware V3.6.0 or later) support the brightness command `![00J0!]`. The value `00` is auto (CDS link) — see [[2.9.2. New and Legacy Command Mapping Table]]
