#### Information we need
To help you faster and more accurately, please prepare the following information.

1. **Customer information**
    - Company name
    - Contact person
    - Phone number
    - Email
2. **Device information**
    - Installation date
    - Controller/converter model name
    - Firmware version
    - LED module information and configuration
3. **Problem details**
    - Photo of how each device is connected
    - Description of the problem
    - Related photos
    - How often the problem occurs
    - Photos of the site
4. **Actions taken and results**
    - What you did
    - Result after the action

#### Repair and inquiry process
The process is simple and clear, to save your time and effort. First, fill in your repair request or inquiry at the link below.

 → [Submit a repair request or inquiry](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd6hTbVWI28uWdFEll4jNNjYpBNcswv3sg-bPXM_bLD9aPohA/viewform) (form in Korean)


#### Solution guidance and knowledge base

Enter all the information, then click the **submit** button. The Dabit Solution technical team checks your inquiry as quickly as possible. We then explain a solution for your situation clearly and in detail.

We document every solution that we provide and store it in our internal database. This helps us respond faster and more accurately when a similar problem occurs again.
