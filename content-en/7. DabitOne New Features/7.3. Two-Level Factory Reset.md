---
title: 7.3. Two-Level Factory Reset
---

> [!abstract] Two-level factory reset — soft / full (new in DabitOne)
> A function to select what to reset: only the content, or everything including the communication settings

#### What is better

- The legacy programs have only one type of factory reset. When you click it, it erases **everything**, including LAN/IP, fonts, and screen size.
- DabitOne divides it into 2 levels.
  - **Soft reset** — erases only the content, and keeps LAN/IP, fonts, and screen size.
  - **Full reset** — keeps only the MAC and password, and resets everything else.

#### How to use

1. In the `고급` (Advanced) tab, click `공장초기화` (Factory reset)
2. Select `소프트` (Soft) or `강력` (Full)
3. Follow the instructions. When you send the command, the controller restarts immediately.

> [!warning] Caution
> A full reset also deletes the communication settings (IP and others). If the device is connected remotely, the connection can drop, so use it carefully. Also, some old firmware versions do not support the soft reset — in that case, DabitOne blocks it for safety.
