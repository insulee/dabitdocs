---
title: 3.12. Keeping Time with the RTC Module (DS3231)
---

> [!abstract] How to connect and set up the RTC module (DS3231) so that the controller keeps the correct time when its power is cut

## 1. Overview

- When the power is cut, the controllers (DB500, DB600, DB502, DB520, DB720) reset their internal time
- When you connect a **DS3231 RTC module**, the controller keeps the correct time after the power comes back
- When you need real-time time data, a [[3.13. Setting Up and Testing a GPS Receiver|GPS receiver]] is the most accurate. Where you cannot use GPS, for example indoors, use the DS3231 RTC module

> [!tip] DB400, DB402 series
> The DB400 and DB402 have an accurate built-in RTC. After you sync them with the PC time, they keep the time without error. But after you turn the power off and on, you must sync the time again. When the clock function is important, we recommend a DB500, DB600, DB502, DB520, or DB720 with a DS3231.

## 2. Supported firmware

- **v3.4.9 or later** is necessary
- Where to check the version
	- DabitOne: `고급` (Advanced) > `펌웨어 업데이트` (Firmware Update) > `펌웨어 읽기` (Read Firmware)
	- DBPS: `설정` (Settings) > `펌웨어 정보 읽기` (Read firmware information)

## 3. Wiring diagram

Connect the DS3231 module to the **RELAY port** of the controller.

| **DS3231 module** | **Controller pin** |
| ------------- | ------------- |
| **GND**       | RELAY GND     |
| **VCC**       | RELAY 5V      |
| **SDA**       | RELAY D1      |
| **SCL**       | RELAY D2      |

> [!note] The wiring is the same for the DB500, DB600, DB502, DB520, and DB720.

## 4. Board function settings

1. Open the board settings and click `읽기` (Read)
	- DabitOne: `고급` (Advanced) > `보드기능설정` (Board settings) > `읽기` (Read)
	- DBPS: `설정` (Settings) > `보드 기능 설정` (Board settings) > `읽기` (Read)
2. For the **J4(4핀) 모드** (J4 (4-pin) mode) item, select **DS3231**
3. Save
	- DabitOne: `전송` (Send)
	- DBPS: `설정` (Set) > `닫기` (Close)

## 5. Setting and checking the time

1. **First time setting**
	- DabitOne: `고급` (Advanced) > `시간설정` (Time settings) > `PC 시간 전송` (Send PC time)
	- DBPS: `설정` (Settings) > `PC 시간 동기화` (Sync PC time)
2. **Check the time**: use a path below, or send the [[2.8.1. ASCII Protocol Manual|ASCII protocol]] command `![0031!]` and check the response
	- DabitOne: `고급` (Advanced) > `시간설정` (Time settings) > `컨트롤러 시간 읽기` (Read controller time)
	- DBPS: `설정` (Settings) > `컨트롤러 시간읽기` (Read controller time)
3. **Check the operation**: cut the controller power and supply it again. Make sure that the time shows correctly without a reset

> [!tip] New controllers (firmware V3.6.0 or later) can read and set the time with the ASCII command `![00K0!]` — see [[2.9.2. New and Legacy Command Mapping Table]]

> [!tip] At sites that can reach an internet time server, the DB520 · DB720 get the current time without a command after you turn the power off and on. At sites without external internet, keep the time with the RTC module or by sending the PC time, as before
