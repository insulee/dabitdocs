title: 3.29. Hardware Settings for 32-Duty Modules
---
> [!abstract] How to set the controller hardware so that Dabit Solution 4 rows × 4 columns (64x64 pixels) 32-duty modules show correctly

#### Modules that this applies to

- **Model:** DABIT_P3_4R4C_32S_900cd(P3(2121)64X64-32S-M5.1
- **Related document:** [[LED Module_P3_64x64_32S_Indoor.pdf]]
#### Controllers that this applies to
- DB600 series controllers
- DB502T controller

#### Hardware settings

##### DB600 series controllers
- **Remove the R74 resistor** at the BH3 port, and the module shows correctly
- ![[32duty_저항제거.png]]

##### DB502T controller
- To show correctly, **bridge the two pads at the R9 position** on the **back** of the controller with solder
- Connect the two separate pads on the PCB with a jumper wire or similar, and solder them
- ![[32duty_R9.png]]
